# Demo FAQ

## Orders and delivery
Use the structured order dataset for order status. No delivery ETA, tracking carrier or cancellation API is connected. An unknown order is not found; ask the customer to check the ID. Do not create or cancel orders.

## Product availability
Prices are mock INR values. Stock, discounts, EMI eligibility and live offers are not supplied. Do not invent them.

## Returns and refunds
No verified return window or refund policy has been supplied. Ask the user to check their invoice and contact the store for eligibility.
